**Plan: Coyoacán – Birdwatching & Gentle Stroll**

**Title:** Coyoacán Birding Escape

**Time:** 90 minutes

**Mood:** Relaxed, contemplative, bird-focused

**Weather:** Code 53, 19.2°C, Humidity 70% – Mild temps, expect rain.

**Nearby OpenStreetMap Spots:**
* Jardín Centenario (garden, ~0.1 km)
* Viveros de Coyoacán (park, ~1.27 km)
* Parque de los Venados (park, ~2.42 km)

**Here’s your plan:**

**1. Initial Exploration (15 mins)** – Start at the Jardín Centenario (Garden). It’s a beautiful, well-maintained garden with plenty of space to relax and observe. Walk slowly, taking in the scenery.

**2. Birding Focus (45 mins)** – Head to Viveros de Coyoacán (Park). Look for various bird species – hummingbirds, tanagers, and swallows are common. Bring binoculars!

**3. Tianguis Time (30 mins)** – Head to a nearby tianguis (market square). Observe the lively atmosphere, sample some street food (try a *tostada*!), and soak in the local energy. It's a great place to chat with vendors.

**4. Gentle Bike Ride (15 mins)** – Find a relatively flat section of the Parque de los Venados (Park). Enjoy a leisurely bike ride – it’s a beautiful route along the river.

**Leave the Phone:** Put your phone in your bag; it’s great for photos, but you want to be present and enjoy the moment!

**Important Note:** Check the weather forecast before you go. If rain is predicted, pack a compact umbrella.
