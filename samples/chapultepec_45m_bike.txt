**Mexico City Outdoor Plan – Chapultepec & Polanco (Rainy Day)**

**Title:** Chapultepec & Polanco: A Relaxing Rainy Bike Ride

**Where to Go:** Chapultepec Park & Polanco neighborhood.

**How to Get There:** Take the Metro Line 8 (Chapultepec Station) – a 15-minute ride. Alternatively, walk (approx. 20 minutes) if you’re staying near the park.

**What to Bring:** Waterproof jacket, umbrella, comfortable walking shoes, small backpack, water bottle, snacks.

**Timeline:** 45 minutes

**Primary Activity:** Bike Ride through Chapultepec Park – Focus on the trails and gardens.

**Here’s a suggested route:** Start at the Chapultepec Park entrance (near the plaza). Cycle along the paved paths towards the *Jardín de Adultos Mayores* (approx. 0.7 km). Take a detour through the *Plaza Uruguay* (1.38 km) for a scenic view. Continue towards the *Parque Lincoln* (1.53 km) and then the *Parque America* (1.86 km).

**Optional Backup (Rain):** If the rain intensifies, head back towards the Plaza Uruguay for shelter.

**Birding:**  The Chapultepec Park is a haven for birds. Look for hummingbirds, tanagers, and swallows.

**Tianguis (Market):**  The Mercado Escondido (approx. 1.58 km) is a fantastic spot for a quick tianguis – browse local crafts and food.

**Tips:**  Take your time, enjoy the scenery, and breathe in the fresh air.  Disconnect from your phone and fully immerse yourself in the experience.

**Leave the Phone:**  Enjoy the moment!
